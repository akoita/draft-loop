## 1. What the service is

DraftLoop produces a job-specific CV draft from career material you supply.
An AI author writes the draft, an independent AI critic from a different
company reviews it, and you decide: you edit, approve and export the document
as Markdown, Word (DOCX) or PDF. Every substantive statement in a draft is
linked to the material it came from, and the service never adds facts,
employers, dates, credentials or figures that your material does not contain.

The service prepares documents. It does not apply to jobs for you, send
messages, publish documents or contact employers.

## 5. What the AI output is, and is not

The drafts are suggestions produced by AI models from your material. They can
contain mistakes: a wrongly placed date, an overstated role, a requirement
read too generously. The service shows the source of each statement and the
critic's findings precisely so that you can check them. You are responsible
for every document you send to an employer; verify it before you do.

Nothing in the service is career, legal or employment advice, and no result
is promised: a tailored CV does not guarantee an interview or a job.
