# Approved Electrician, Commercial — Halden Building Services (fictional)

A building services contractor needs an electrician for fit-out and
maintenance work in offices and retail units.

Requirements:

- [r1] Level 3 electrical qualification and AM2.
- [r2] Current 18th Edition (BS 7671) certificate.
- [r3] Experience installing and testing commercial lighting and power.
- [r4] EV charge point installation experience.
- [r5] Qualified to carry out periodic inspection and testing (EICR).
- [r6] Experience leading a team of electricians on site.
