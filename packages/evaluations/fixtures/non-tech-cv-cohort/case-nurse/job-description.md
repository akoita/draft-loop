# Senior Staff Nurse (Band 6), Cardiology — Brookmere Hospitals Trust (fictional)

The cardiology ward is recruiting an experienced nurse to lead shifts and
support staff development.

Requirements:

- [r1] Current registration as an adult nurse.
- [r2] Experience caring for acutely unwell or cardiac patients, including telemetry.
- [r3] Experience leading a shift as nurse in charge.
- [r4] Supervise and assess students and junior staff.
- [r5] Hold a current Advanced Life Support (ALS) certificate.
- [r6] Hold an independent non-medical prescribing qualification.
