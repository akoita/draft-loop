How it works

Write, check, decide. Then do it again until it is right.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your career

    Add your CV, notes or a short conversation. Redakloop turns them into facts you confirm.

  2. 2

    Add the job you want

    Paste the job description or its link. We list what the employer asks for.

  3. 3

    The author writes

    A Claude model drafts a CV that matches each requirement to your real evidence.

  4. 4

    The critic checks

    A model from another company flags weak, vague or unsupported claims.

  5. 5

    You decide and download

    Accept or reject each finding, approve the draft and export it as PDF or Word.
