[ How the loop works ]

Two AIs from two companies

Many AI tools pad a CV to sound impressive. DraftLoop gives the draft to a second model from another company, whose only job is to find claims your documents do not support.

Model 01 // The writer

Drafts for the role

Reads the job and your career documents, then drafts a CV and a cover letter in the language and tone the role asks for.

Model 02 // The critic

Checks every claim

Compares each claim, number and date with your sources. Anything it cannot trace is flagged and sent back for another draft.

[ Principles ]

Trust comes from the truth

Four rules every draft follows.

Honest

Every line traces back to your real, documented experience. Nothing is invented.

Precise

Drafts match what the job asks for, without padding or empty buzzwords.

Private

Hosted in the EU. Your documents are encrypted, and you can delete them any time.

Yours to decide

You review, edit and approve every document. DraftLoop never sends anything to an employer.
